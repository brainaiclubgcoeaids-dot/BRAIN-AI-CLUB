Our Purpose

A Community Built
For Builders

BRAIN AI CLUB gives students a platform to learn, build, innovate, and connect with the evolving technology ecosystem through workshops, hackathons, and real-world projects.

Our Vision

To build a dynamic community of technology enthusiasts who continuously learn, innovate, solve real-world problems, and contribute to technological and social progress. We aim to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry demands.

Our Mission

  • Promote practical, industry-oriented learning
  • Encourage research, innovation, and projects
  • Organize workshops, hackathons, and activities
  • Develop future-ready technical skills

🧠🚀 BRAIN AI CLUB | OUR FIRST TECHNICAL INITIATIVE

✨ Learning. Coding. Building. Empowering. ✨

We are proud to successfully conduct our first technical workshop – “PYTHON FOR EVERYONE”, organized by the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science & BRAIN AI CLUB at GF’s Godavari College of Engineering & Polytechnic, Jalgaon. 🎓💻

🐍 The session was much more than just learning Python! Students gained valuable insights into:

  • 🔹 💻 Python Programming & Practical Concepts
  • 🔹 🏭 Industry-Oriented Knowledge & Real-World Applications
  • 🔹 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • 🔹 📊 Data Science & Emerging Technologies
  • 🔹 🚀 Industry Requirements & Future-Ready Skills
  • 🔹 🧩 Problem-Solving & Logical Thinking
  • 🔹 🎯 Career Paths & Opportunities in Technology
  • 🔹 💡 Practical Learning & Real-World Projects

🙏 Special thanks to Prajwal Sonawane Sir (Trainer / CEO, Stack Flow Classes, Jalgaon) for delivering an insightful and engaging session and sharing valuable industry-oriented knowledge, practical insights, real-world applications, and career perspectives with our students. 🏭💡

👨‍💻 Yash Chaudhari also actively contributed to the session by guiding students, conducting an interactive doubt-solving session, and sharing valuable insights about career paths, required skills, and opportunities in AI, ML, Data Science and related technologies. 🚀📚

  • 👩‍🏫 Prof. Reshma Attarde — BRAIN AI CLUB Mentor
    For her continuous mentoring, guidance and encouragement. 🌟
  • 👨‍💼 Dr. Anilkumar Vishwakarma — HOD, AI & DS Department
    For his valuable guidance, encouragement and support in providing us the opportunity to conduct such technical initiatives. 🙏
  • 👨‍💼 Dr. Tushar Koli — Academic Dean
    For his constant encouragement and support towards student-driven technical activities. 🚀
  • 👨‍💼 Dr. Vijaykumar Patil — Principal

🚀 This is just the beginning!

We look forward to organizing many more technical workshops, industry interactions, hands-on sessions, project-based learning activities, and career-oriented initiatives for students. 🤖💻🔥
